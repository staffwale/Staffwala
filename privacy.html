Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

What we collect

Name, mobile number, city, role, age and vehicle (partners), booking details (date, time, address, guests, notes), wallet and payout records, bank or UPI details of partners for payouts, reviews, and payment records.

Why we use it

To run your account, show bookings, connect customers with partners, process subscription payments and payouts, prevent fraud and give support.

Who sees what

A partner who accepts a booking sees the customer name, mobile number and address for that booking. Customers with an active subscription can see partner mobile numbers. Customer names in staff history are shortened. Admins can see data needed to verify partners and process payouts.

Payments

Subscription payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not store card or UPI PIN details. We keep order and payment ids.

Storage

Data is stored with Supabase. We keep it while your account is active and as required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your data by contacting us. Some records may be kept for legal and accounting reasons.
