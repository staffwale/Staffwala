Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Subscription

The INR 99 / 30 day subscription is generally non-refundable once activated. If your payment was debited but the subscription did not activate, or you were charged twice, contact us within 7 days and we will activate it or refund the amount to the original payment method within 5-7 working days.

Bookings

Booking amount is paid online at the time of booking. If you cancel before a partner accepts, you get a full refund. After a partner accepts, you can cancel and get a full refund up to one day before the booking date; on or after the booking date please contact us. Refunds go to the original payment method in 5-7 working days. If a partner cancels, the booking is offered to other partners.

Partner wallet

If a payout request is rejected, the amount returns to the partner wallet.
